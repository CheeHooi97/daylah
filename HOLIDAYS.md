# Holiday verification

Checked on 4 October 2026 against government publications:

- Malaysia: [2026 federal and state schedule](https://www.kabinet.gov.my/storage/2025/08/HKA-2026.pdf) and [2027 federal and state schedule](https://www.kabinet.gov.my/storage/2026/08/HKA_2027.pdf), linked from the [BKPP portal](https://www.kabinet.gov.my/hari-kelepasan-am/).
- Singapore: MOM [2026](https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/press-releases/2025/0616-public-holidays-for-2026) and [2027](https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/press-releases/2026/0618-public-holidays-for-2027) announcements.

Singapore includes all 11 scheduled holiday dates per year and the published Sunday replacement dates. MOM's 2026 Hari Raya dates retain the announcement's confirmation caveat.

Malaysia includes all 49 entries in the combined 2026 schedule and all 50 entries in the combined 2027 schedule, including state and territory holidays. The state filter uses explicit applicability for all 16 jurisdictions. Chinese New Year day two is covered nationwide when federal and state entries are combined; Hari Raya Haji day two applies in Kedah, Kelantan, Perlis and Terengganu; Deepavali excludes Sarawak. Special additional gazettes are outside this annual schedule list. Replacement days vary by state and are not automatically inferred. Dates marked subject to change retain that caveat. This is a verified transcription of the published schedules, not a guarantee against future government amendments.

Past holiday dates remain visible for reference but cannot be used as presets. Today is allowed. The click handler and editor save both recheck the date in the holiday country's timezone. Observed dates are checked independently. Holiday presets create one-time events in the country's timezone.

Backend and frontend JSON copies are kept identical. Recheck government updates before extending coverage or changing provisional dates.

## Longer-break suggestions

Selecting a holiday opens break options with 0–2 leave days, including equally long alternatives before and after the holiday, exact leave dates and total consecutive days off. Applicable holidays and published observed dates count as days off; unverified replacement days do not. Users may enter an additional employer-confirmed day off.

For the supported 2026/2027 years, weekend defaults are Friday–Saturday in Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu and Saturday–Sunday in the other states, federal territories and Singapore. Changing the selected state resets the planner to that state's default. Users can override it for their employer's roster. Johor changed to Saturday–Sunday from 1 January 2025, as documented by [MKN](https://www.mkn.gov.my/web/ms/2024/12/31/2025-johor-cuti-hujung-minggu-sabtu-ahad-antara-perkara-baharu-berkuat-kuasa-esok/). State workweeks are documented in [JPA's working hours schedule](https://docs.jpa.gov.my/docs/myppsm/PPSM/SR/Waktu-bekerja/15/).
