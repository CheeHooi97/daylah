Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: 6 October 2026

Your saved dates and private notes stay on your device. DayLah lets you calculate dates and keep personal countdowns without creating an account.

This policy applies to the DayLah Android app and website. The Android app's package name is my.daylah.app.

Information you use in DayLah Storage and deletion Connections and external services Privacy questions

Information you use in DayLah

DayLah processes the dates you enter and your selected timezone on your device to calculate date differences, ages, anniversaries and countdowns. The app can read your device's timezone setting to choose an initial timezone; it does not use GPS or request your location.

When you save an event, DayLah stores its title, date, timezone, repeat setting, colour, private notes and saved time locally. This information is used to display and manage your saved dates. DayLah does not upload your saved events, birth dates or private notes to its servers in this release.

DayLah does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics trackers or third-party crash reporting. It does not sell your personal information or use your saved dates for advertising.

Storage and deletion

The Android app keeps saved events in its local app storage. The website uses your browser's local storage for saved events and may cache app files and holiday calendars to support offline use. DayLah does not use advertising cookies or provide cloud sync for saved events.

Saved events remain on your device until you delete them or clear DayLah's storage. To delete an event and its notes, choose Delete beside the event in Your dates.

  • Android: clear DayLah's app data in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove its local saved data. Android app backup is disabled.
  • Website: clear the site data for DayLah in your browser settings to remove saved events and cached files.

Deleted local data cannot be restored by the developer. DayLah's current release does not offer public countdown sharing; no public event is created when you save a date.

Connections and external services

Date calculations and saved events work offline. The Android app bundles its app files and holiday calendars. The website downloads those files from its host. Visiting the website or an external link sends ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and browser request details, to the service you visit. Your saved events and private notes are not included in those requests.

Holiday source links open government websites. Those websites have their own privacy policies. Google Play and Android may process information when you download, update or use their services under their own policies.

If you choose to copy a result, DayLah writes the result to your device's clipboard. Clipboard access by other apps is governed by your operating system. DayLah does not read your clipboard in the background.

The Android app has internet permission for opening online content. It does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera or microphone.

Children's privacy

DayLah does not ask children to submit personal information to the developer. Dates and notes entered in the app stay on the device as described above.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its last updated date when DayLah's data handling changes. If an update introduces online sharing or another feature that sends personal information off your device, the policy will describe that feature before it is made available.

Privacy questions

For questions about this policy or DayLah's handling of your information, contact the developer using the support email under App support in DayLah's Google Play listing.

If you contact the developer, your email address and the information you choose to send will be used to respond to your request. Please do not include private event details unless they are needed to explain your question.
